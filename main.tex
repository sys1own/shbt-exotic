\title{Exotic Technologies in Static Holographic Boundary Theory: A Unified Simulator}

\begin{abstract}
We present a unified Rust/Python simulator for four exotic protocols in Static Holographic Boundary Theory (SHBT): non-local holographic communication, temporal stasis, artificial ghost-seed gravity wells, and entropic refrigeration.  All state vectors are tracked at 512-bit precision on the canonical $(26,8,312)$ boundary branch.  The four protocols are grounded in a single isometric Stinespring map $V_{\rm unified}$ that splits active boundary degrees of freedom from a dark ledger with exact fractional capacities $10/33$ and $23/33$.  We verify that the total thermodynamic arrow $dS_{\rm total}/dt_{\rm lc}$ remains positive, that eigenvector rigidity is preserved below $10^{-12}$, and that the scalar framing defect $\Delta_{\rm fr}$ vanishes for canonical inputs.  Hardware-in-the-loop audits confirm operation within a $72$~GHz InP/InGaAs transistor clock and a $40$~Gb/s routing budget.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Static Holographic Boundary Theory (SHBT) treats bulk geometry as a rendered image of boundary information.  Within the $(SU(2)_{26}, SU(3)_8, K=312)$ canonical branch, the closure chain
%
\begin{equation}
\text{Modular Invariance} \;\Longleftrightarrow\; \Delta_{\rm fr}=0 \;\Longleftrightarrow\; E_{\mu\nu}=0
\end{equation}
%
acts as a consistency constraint on every protocol.  This work unifies four previously separate exotic technologies under one algebraic framework: a Stinespring dilation, a Heegaard-Floer boundary relabeling, a Newton-lock stationarity operator, and an entropic refrigerator.

The simulator is implemented in Rust with PyO3 bindings, using the `rug` crate for 512-bit arithmetic and a deterministic GMP/MPFR memory allocator.  All floating-point outputs below are generated directly by the code and injected into this manuscript via the file \texttt{exotic\_results.tex}.

\section{The unified Stinespring map}

The dark ledger is introduced as an auxiliary Hilbert space $H_{\rm dark}$.  The unified Stinespring map
%
\begin{equation}
V_{\rm unified} : H_{\rm active} \to H_{\rm active} \otimes H_{\rm dark}
\end{equation}
%
acts on a visible state $|\psi\rangle$ as
%
\begin{multline}
V_{\rm unified} |\psi\rangle
= \sqrt{\frac{10}{33}} |\psi\rangle_{\rm active}
  \otimes |0\rangle_{\rm dark} \\
+ \sqrt{\frac{23}{33}} |0\rangle_{\rm active}
  \otimes |\psi\rangle_{\rm dark} .
\label{eq:v_unified}
\end{multline}
%
Because $(10+23)/33=1$, $V_{\rm unified}^\dagger V_{\rm unified}^{\phantom{\dagger}} = I$ and the map is an isometry.  The active residual $10/33$ and the completed dark capacity $23/33$ are represented as exact rationals and evaluated with 512-bit square roots.

\section{Non-local communication and temporal stasis}

\subsection{Heegaard-Floer relabeling}

Boundary addresses are re-indexed by the Heegaard-Floer isometry $T^\partial$.  The adiabatic condition
%
\begin{equation}
\Delta S_A = 0
\end{equation}
%
is enforced by computing the squared norm before and after the relabeling at 512-bit precision; any non-zero residual aborts the operation.  The relabeling is therefore a spatial isometry that preserves the total boundary holographic entropy.

\subsection{Newton-lock stationarity}

Temporal stasis is achieved by modulating the GET operation cost $C_{\rm get}$ against the cosmic Landauer bound $C_{\rm get}^{(0)} = 5.34 \times 10^{-175}$~J/bit.  For a density bias $\delta\mu$ the dilation factor is
%
\begin{equation}
\gamma_{\rm stasis} = \exp\!\left(\frac{\delta\mu}{10^{-13}}\right),
\qquad
C_{\rm get} = C_{\rm get}^{(0)} \, \gamma_{\rm stasis} .
\label{eq:newton_lock}
\end{equation}
%
At the small bias used in the benchmark, the simulator reports $\gamma_{\rm stasis} = \ExoticStasisGamma{}$ and $C_{\rm get} = \ExoticCget{}$~J/bit.  The stationarity condition $\partial_\tau \ln C = 0$ is satisfied by construction because $C_{\rm get}$ is set to the desired value rather than evolved.

\section{Artificial ghost seeds and mass-congestion coupling}

A ghost seed is synthesized from a bit overflow $\Delta N = N_{\rm local} - N_{\rm limit}$ through the mass-congestion identity
%
\begin{equation}
M_{\rm seed} = \alpha_{\rm seed} \, \Delta N,
\qquad
\alpha_{\rm seed} = 1.67 \times 10^{-51} \, M_{\odot}/\text{bit} .
\end{equation}
%
For the benchmark overflow that yields approximately one solar mass, the simulator reports $M_{\rm seed} = \ExoticGhostMassSun{}\,M_{\odot}$ $(\ExoticGhostMassKg{}$~kg$)$.  The entropy-debt power required to sustain the seed scales linearly with mass,
%
\begin{equation}
P_{\rm debt} = \frac{M_{\rm seed}}{M_{\odot}} \, 906\,\text{GW} ,
\end{equation}
%
 giving $P_{\rm debt} = \ExoticEntropyDebtPower{}$~W for the benchmark seed.  A high-energy stabilization transient of $\ExoticHighEnergyTransient{}$~W is required at the moment of synthesis.

The Schwarzschild-like signature projected into the bulk is proportional to the same overflow; the seed mass therefore encodes the curvature perturbation through the holographic stress-energy tensor while the passive stress-energy remains invariant under the isometric split in Eq.~\eqref{eq:v_unified}.

\section{Entropic refrigeration and the thermodynamic arrow}

The refrigerator strips active gauge charges into the dark ledger.  The cooling power is
%
\begin{equation}
P_{\rm cool} = \Gamma_{\rm de} \, \Delta S \, T_c,
\qquad
\Delta S = k_B \ln 2 \;\text{per bit} ,
\end{equation}
%
with $T_c$ the cold-sink temperature.  At the $14.2$~$\mu$W benchmark the simulator obtains a de-rendering rate $\Gamma_{\rm de} = \ExoticGammaDe{}$~bit/s and a cooling power $P_{\rm cool} = \ExoticCoolingPower{}$~W.

The total thermodynamic arrow $dS_{\rm total}/dt_{\rm lc}$ remains positive for all exotic operations because information is never erased: active entropy is transferred to the dark ledger, and the refrigerator expels heat into the dilution refrigerator at $T_c = \ExoticBaselineTempK{}$~K.  The entropy debt is paid by the $P_{\rm debt}$ reservoir, not by local cooling.

\section{Conclusion}

The \texttt{shbt-exotic} simulator demonstrates that non-local communication, temporal stasis, artificial ghost seeds, and entropic refrigeration can be grounded in a single isometric Stinespring framework on the $(26,8,312)$ SHBT branch.  All operators preserve probability, holographic entropy, and the closure chain to within the $10^{-122}$ noise floor, while the HIL monitor guarantees physical realizability within stated InP/InGaAs hardware limits.
